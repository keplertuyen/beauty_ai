# /discovery
**Purpose:** capture requirements correctly before any build (Delivery Process §5).
**Inputs:** workshop transcript / discovery notes.
**Steps:**
1. Produce: business goals, personas, workflows, FR draft (FR-001…), NFR draft (NFR-001…), domain rules, data entities, integrations, assumptions, open questions, out-of-scope.
2. Mark inferences as Assumption; never put architecture into requirements.
**Output:** structured requirement notes + open-questions log.
**Approval gate:** BA + customer confirm scope/priority.
**Forbidden:** adding requirements not in the notes; deciding architecture.
