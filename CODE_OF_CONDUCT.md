# Code of Conduct

Syntheprise Framework is an open-source project (Apache-2.0) originated at Syntheprise and built with
its community. We keep collaboration professional, respectful, and focused on
quality.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and constructive in reviews, issues, and discussions.
- Critique the work, not the person. Assume good intent; disagree with evidence.
- Welcome questions — a clear question is cheaper than a wrong assumption.
- Respect others' privacy; never share private or client data through the project.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Knowingly committing secrets, malicious code, or unsafe changes.
- Dismissing review or quality gates to "just ship it."

## Scope
Applies to all repository spaces (issues, pull requests, commits, discussions)
and to related internal channels when referencing this project.

## Enforcement
Report concerns to the repository owner or **tm@syntheprise.ai**. Reports are
handled discreetly. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and
may revoke access for behavior that violates this policy.

Adapted, in spirit, from the Contributor Covenant for an internal team context.
